# Spicy Rodents — Roblox Rebuild Brief: Addendum A (from the owner, 2026-11-09)

These requirements extend `reference/Roblox_Rebuild_Brief.pdf`. Where they conflict, this addendum wins.
The owner's words come first, then the working interpretation. Items marked **(confirm)** are open with the owner.

## A1. Police can drive

> "make it so that the top two cops can actually play driving cars and getting in and out of cars, plus radar and sightings showing up on map."

- The **two highest-ranked police players** on the server get a drivable patrol car each. They can enter and exit it (proximity prompt / key), drive it (throttle, brake, steer, siren/lights toggle) and leave it parked anywhere.
- Other officers move on foot. AI patrol cars keep running for AI officers. **(confirm)**
- Player-driven cars take part in the same server-side **swept traffic collision**, so they can run over the spicy rodent. They also obey the traffic sim's yielding where it matters for AI cars around them.
- **Radar and sightings on the map:**
  - There is a town map (minimap plus a full-screen map) with marker layers.
  - **Sightings:** when an AI or player human spots the spicy rodent, or a bite happens, a sighting marker appears at that spot for police (and hospital?) players. It fades over time. The drone's spot-and-mark gives a precise, short-lived marker. **(confirm who sees sightings)**
  - **Radar:** a police-car radar shows nearby speeders and moving vehicles. Speed readouts feed the ticket side quests (A2).

## A2. Promotion through work, side quests and kicks

> "the promotions in rank with a successful kick, or successful work at hospital or with police station, which can just be fighting crime, insert, side quests of settling petty argument or writing up fender bender, or giving parking ticket or speeding ticket or running stop sign ticket, or stopping buggler [burglar], likewise, in working fast and retrieving the right medicine or instruments in hospital environment"

Rank (the `Roles` ladder) rises from:
- **A successful kick** on the spicy rodent, by any human (vigilante behaviour is rewarded).
- **Police work** = fighting crime, plus side quests generated by the town simulation:
  - **Settle a petty argument:** two AI townspeople arguing. Approach them and pick the right calming response or mediate.
  - **Write up a fender bender:** AI cars have minor collisions. Inspect both cars, talk to both drivers and file the report.
  - **Parking ticket:** AI cars parked illegally (hydrant, red curb, driveway, overtime).
  - **Speeding ticket:** caught on radar, pull over (lights/siren) and issue the ticket.
  - **Running a stop sign:** the town needs stop signs and AI drivers who sometimes roll through them.
  - **Stop a burglar:** a burglar AI breaks into a house. Catch them before they escape.
- **Hospital work** = speed and correctness:
  - Patients and staff need a specific **medicine or instrument**. Fetch the right one from the pharmacy or supply room under time pressure. The wrong item costs time and score.
  - Also disinfecting infected humans (brief section 9).
- Play runs **live across in-game days**. Players can pursue rank **or roam freely and do whatever they want**. No quest is mandatory.
- The brief's credits keep working: work earns credits, and credits raise queue rank and buy upgrades.

## A3. Avatar blending, 70% stock / 30% player avatar

> "their avatars should be a blend between the character they assume, even animals, and the preset stock specific character, 70% stock character 30% Roblox avatar, with creative innovations if the avatar is particularly bizarre be creative and use user design on the character reconciled with basic patterns and colors"

When a player possesses an actor, the actor's look is **70% the stock role character and 30% the player's own Roblox avatar**:
- **Humans:** keep the role's body type, uniform and silhouette (cop, nurse, townsperson). Take from the avatar: skin tone, hair or one signature head accessory, face, and avatar colours as accent colours (trim, tie, scarf, shoes).
- **Animals (squirrel, spicy rodent, fox, deer):** keep the species model and its stock markings. Take from the avatar: its dominant colours tinted into the fur (capped blend), patterns (stripes, patches, tail tip) coloured from the avatar's shirt/pants colours, eye colour, and one avatar accessory shrunk onto the head socket.
- **Bizarre avatars** (non-human bundles, extreme proportions, neon colours, huge accessories): be creative but keep the role readable.
  - Clamp saturation and brightness to the town palette.
  - Pick one signature feature, for example a neon avatar becomes a squirrel with neon-tipped ears and tail.
  - Drop anything that breaks silhouette or hitboxes.
- **Technical notes:**
  - Read the avatar server-side with `Players:GetHumanoidDescriptionFromUserId`.
  - Body colours and accessory IDs are available. Reading pixels of other users' clothing images is likely restricted, so derive colours from body colours, accessory metadata, or a curated mapping. **(feasibility to verify)**
  - The blend is reversible: when the player leaves, the AI-driven actor returns to 100% stock.

## Addendum C (owner, 2026-11-10)

- **C1. A bite always ends at the hospital.** Owner: "a person bit just ran away, there should be an ambulance come and a crowd gather sometimes, sometimes the symptoms should show up a bit later, but it should always be that they have to go to the hospital to get cured".
  - A bitten person is infected and runs. **Sometimes** they collapse a short way off: a scene opens, an ambulance is called to the nearest road point, 2 to 4 people from nearby gather and watch, the crew collects the patient and drives them to the hospital. **Sometimes** they get away looking fine and the symptoms show 40 to 120 seconds later (red eyes, a stagger); they then walk in to the hospital themselves or collapse and get an ambulance.
  - **The only cure is the hospital.** Nobody recovers on their own in town. At the hospital a patient is admitted, treated (30 seconds, tunable) and released cured back into the town.
  - **This supersedes brief v2 §8 for humans** (V2-36: "cured instantly if recently bitten" when the rodent dies). `Tunables infection.recentBiteSecs` stays but defaults to 0: a rodent's death cures nobody.
  - A person a player is holding is never frozen, hidden or moved: they are told on screen what happened and are cured by walking into the hospital and staying inside for the treatment time.
- **C2. Townspeople say worried things.** Owner: "make sure some of the people say, oh my god, its one of those squirrels again, and various other phrases of concern". Some of the people who spot the Spicy Rodent (or an infected squirrel), witness a bite, stand in the crowd or crew the ambulance say a short line in a speech bubble: exactly "Oh my god, it's one of those squirrels again!" and others like it, mild (nothing stronger than "Oh my god"). Not everyone speaks at once: one line per person per 12 seconds, at most three lines in town per 5 seconds. The lines live in `src/shared/Speech/Lines.luau`.
