05.4 / LIVING TOWNMORE LIFE ABOVE THE STREETS.Apple trees, nesting birds, distant planes, and new traffic.

Run through the streets, leap between trees and telephone wires, sneak up on people, and escape the neighbors who fight back. Explore the houses, park, cemetery, shops, estates, and hospital. Follow the eastern road past the shops to Willow River and Bellwether Quay: a stone bridge, riverside market, and an old town of timber-framed houses. Beyond the market, Bellwether Galleria has six walk-in shops, twin stairs to an upper gallery, collectible coins, and a wishing fountain. Walk through the automatic doors; press E beside the fountain to toss one Squirrel Coin. Look for apple trees and nests, birds in flight, and an occasional silent airplane with a short white trail. Dump trucks and RVs now travel the streets.

How to play, climbing & more controls ⌄

Climbing & movement

Hold W as you approach a tree trunk, or press E beside it to grab on. Use W/S to climb or descend. Select branches and wire junctions with A/D, then press E to transfer. Q drops you from an elevated route.

Stealth & survival

Hold the right mouse button to stalk, then left-click to pounce. Unaware people are easier to surprise. People may evade or defend themselves after an attack. Being run over by a moving vehicle ends your run; jump clear or travel above the street. P pauses the game.

Places & objectives

Find eight hidden chili peppers for temporary speed boosts. Bites earn Power and Squirrel Coins; Power decays after long stretches without a bite. Sycamore Park and Cedar Rest Cemetery are north; the hospital is west of the park, while older homes, estates, stores, and other locations spread across town. Some doors, garages, and vents use E.

Display & sound

Choose Golden Hour, Clear Day, or Blue Hour lighting in Display. Auto graphics adjusts detail to your device. Online detail adds bark textures, sky reflections and soft light bloom; the built-in world is always available.

M toggles the HUD, TAB cycles layouts, K chooses visible panels, and H toggles the control legend; C recenters the camera; L shows diagnostics; G opens display settings. Use the VOICES button to mute or enable speech. ESC releases the mouse cursor.
