PINBALL ASSET VALIDATION

Result
------
The unmodified supplied check_glb.py reports: 24 of 24 files pass.
The supplemental audit reports: 24 of 24 files pass.
The assets consist of 21 GLB models and three backglass PNG images.

Measured assembled geometry
---------------------------
Acorn Attack:   15,304 triangles
Spicy Rodent:   14,194 triangles
Space Squirrel: 15,696 triangles
These totals include one cabinet, each machine-specific file, two flippers,
three pop bumpers, one plunger and one gate. Game-generated balls, lamps,
rollovers, score text and other runtime objects are outside these totals.
Every model also stays within its individual triangle budget.

Mechanical contract
-------------------
The supplied checker validates the original required names and world origins,
reference contact footprints, height bounds and file-specific triangle limits.
Every required reference object is present. The maximum measured origin error
is 0.00002899 mm, against the allowed 1 mm.

The supplemental audit also verifies applied unit scale, one material and one
primitive per mesh object, at most ten extra Deco_ objects per file, embedded
resources, and no exported cameras, lights, skins or animations. Contact objects
are not shorter than their references, within a 0.01 mm numeric tolerance.
All playfield objects other than ramps stay below the 0.14 m glass plane.

All 432 sampled positions on the original upward-facing ramp track
surfaces are retained within the supplemental 1 mm threshold. This checks the
original riding surfaces as well as the supplied checker's overall footprint.

Artwork and materials
---------------------
Each Board carries one embedded 486 x 1024 image. Apron images are embedded and
remain within the 1024-pixel limit. Each backglass PNG is exactly 854 x 1024.
Only Board and Apron meshes carry image textures. No normal, roughness,
occlusion or emissive texture maps are exported. Glass and ramp tracks are the
only transparent objects; Glass uses alpha blend at approximately 5% opacity.

Source review confirms that all 48 painted labels and 74 lamp-ring centres
are drawn directly from pinball_data.json. Board UVs map the back of the table
to the top of each image. Backglass artwork preserves dark, subdued areas in
all five reserved score/text bands and contains no baked live scoreboard text.

Visual review
-------------
The three Board textures, three Backglass images, cabinet detail and assembled
hardware were inspected. The three themes use original woodland, heat and
space illustrations. No gambling imagery, protected red-cross emblem, green
rodent, gore or profanity was observed. The FREE PLAY cabinet text is legible
and contains no coin slot artwork.

Visible overlapping rail surfaces found in early previews were rebuilt as
continuous exterior surfaces. The final hardware review shows clean arches,
OAK-guide corners and plastic-guide junctions without the earlier black facets.
The final three-machine presentation render and all three assembled top-down
playfield renders were also reviewed. Cabinet joins are clean, the table art
is upright and legible, and the finished guide surfaces no longer show the
earlier overlap artifacts. No further visual correction was identified.

Practical limits
----------------
This is a visual asset package for the already-existing pinball game. It does
not replace ball physics, controls, collisions, lamps, rollovers or scoreboard
logic. The supplied pack contains no executable Roblox project, so interactive
play, Roblox import behaviour and phone frame rates have not been tested here.
Triangle counts are a geometry budget check, not a device-performance benchmark.
