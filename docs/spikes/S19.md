# S19 Body budget at brief v2's floors (the re-scoped S5)

**Verdict: GO** for the server-script budget. Up to 160 human Humanoids:
- AI per frame stays at or under 0.13 ms;
- the whole frame (AI, a 16-bot paintball match, BulkMoveTo of every rig) stays at or under 0.65 ms;
- BulkMoveTo of 150 rigs takes 0.32-0.36 ms.

No D4 fallback is needed. **D23's hard cap of 90 stands**: the script budget does not bind at that
size, and what remains to measure (Humanoid physics and state, replication, rendering) needs the live
MicroProfiler (OS-1a, W7, F-14).

## Question
Does the AI and body budget hold at brief v2's worst case, with about 90 or more human Humanoids? (BUILD_PLAN §4.3 S19, §1.5, D4, D23, D25.)

## Method
`tests/engine/BodyBudget.luau` (task `tools/cloud/tasks/bodybudget_s19.luau`; one Luau Execution task, CI place v9 with `--inject`).

**Each variant builds:**
- **the S5 scenario** (`src/server/AI/Scenario`, unchanged): AI humans on posts, 8 non-Humanoid riders, 14 squirrels, 1 rodent, 6 deer, 1 fox, 45 vehicles at 30 Hz, 2 fender benders and 49 moving focus points;
- **49 human-player bodies** riding the focus points;
- **a 16-bot paintball match** (`src/server/Paintball/Harness`, bots only, 8 v 8, shooting and taking the stairs).

**Every body is a Humanoid puppet:** an anchored root with Motor6D limbs, and `BulkMoveTo` on the roots. Humanoids were counted in the workspace.

**Each variant then:**
- runs 1,200 frames at 60 Hz;
- benchmarks BulkMoveTo on exactly 150 rigs (25 repetitions, median);
- records memory.

**AI humans and the protected posts.** The scenario keeps the S5 config's protected posts at 31:
- 14 police, with the guard and the car crew (brief floor >= 6);
- 14 hospital staff (floor >= 10);
- 3 ambulance crew.

The other AI humans are townspeople, shop workers and attraction staff.

**S5 is untouched.** `tests/engine/AIScale.luau` and its three scenarios are unchanged, and the S5 Lune tests are green.

**Build, memory and liveness:**
- Building took 31-51 ms per variant.
- Memory grew about 40 KB per rig: 6.5-10 MB per variant by `GetTotalMemoryUsageMb`.
- The Luau heap stayed at 2.6-4.6 MB.
- Each variant had 0 stuck actors, 0 NaN and 0 outside the clamp. The 16 bots fired 367 balls with 0 stuck.
- The whole task took 2.9 s of engine time.

**One outlier.** A single 16 ms frame appeared in `s19_109_worst` (max; p99 0.73 ms), in both AI and move at once. It looks like a GC or host hiccup and did not repeat in the larger variants.

## What this does not measure
Luau Execution runs no physics, has no players and does no replication. So these are still unmeasured:
- the Humanoid state machine and physics step: the puppets are anchored, and `EvaluateStateMachine` is off in production;
- replication of about 110 moving bodies to 49 clients;
- client rendering.

Those set the real cap. The OS-1a Telemetry overlay checks client receive at about 50 KB/s, and W7 runs the live MicroProfiler (F-14).
