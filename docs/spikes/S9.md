# S9: Avatar APIs (70/30 blend inputs)

**Question** (BUILD_PLAN §4.3 S9; D13; Addendum A3/B1; brief v2 §13a). In Luau Execution: do
`Players:GetHumanoidDescriptionFromUserId` and `Players:CreateHumanoidModelFromDescription` return
traits and models for public UserIds (dynamic heads and layered clothing included)? Does
`MarketplaceService:GetProductInfo` give colour words for their items, and at what rate? Can a classic
face Decal be copied onto a custom head and a rigid accessory welded to a `HeadSocket` on a
non-Humanoid rig? Do 60 descriptions fetch in under 3 s? Pass: traits returned; colour words for most
items (rate recorded); face and accessory transfer work; compose works on every species; 60 < 3 s.
Fallback: verify in the FRB; body and accessory colours plus the curated table; stock face.

**Verdict: GO, with two facts that change D13's face path.** Everything the APIs must do works
headlessly and fast (16/16 descriptions, 16/16 R15 models, 60 distinct descriptions in 1.25 s,
accessory welded to `HeadSocket`, classic faces loadable). But (1) every one of the 16 accounts came
back with a **dynamic head** (`FaceControls`, `Face = 0`), including the 2009-era staff accounts,
so the "copy the classic face Decal from the created model" step has no source any more; the face
path becomes "classic `Face` id (when non-zero) through `InsertService:LoadAsset` -> Decal onto
`FaceDecal`, else a stock expression", which was tested and works. (2) Colour words in item names hit
**42 %** (16 of 38), not "most": body colours and the curated `item_colours.json` carry the rest.

## Method

`tests/engine/AvatarApi.luau`, run twice through `tools/cloud run_engine_tests --inject` on the CI
place (version 1); results in `build/reports/avatar_api.json` and `avatar_api2.json`. For each
UserId: fetch the description, read every property the blend needs plus `GetAccessories(true)`,
`GetProductInfo` on shirt, pants, graphic t-shirt and every accessory (cached per asset), create the
R15 model, inspect `Head` (class, children, `FaceControls`, Decal), run `BlendRules.traits` and
`BlendRules.compose` for human and squirrel on the live data, try the face and accessory transfers,
then time 60 fetches. Every call is pcall-wrapped and recorded.

**UserIds used** (public, long-lived accounts plus the owner): 1 Roblox, 156 builderman, 261
Shedletsky, 2 John Doe, 3 Jane Doe, 16 Clockwork, 21557 erik.cassel, 80254 stickmasterluke, 1311,
23415609, 13645, 11958930616 (the experience owner), 1234567890, 2000000000, 3000000000, 4000000000
(arbitrary later ids to catch newer avatar features). Timing set: 60 distinct ids 107, 114, ..., 520.
Classic faces for the LoadAsset path: 144075659 (Winning Smile) and 8560971.

## BlendRules (`src/shared/Avatar/BlendRules.luau`, pure, 14 Lune tests)

- `mix(stock, avatar, 0.3)` is the 70/30; `clampPalette` holds S <= 0.55 and V in 0.39-0.91 (tested on
  500 random colours); `clampAccent` (S <= 0.8, V <= 0.95) is the softer clamp for the one neon accent.
- `traits(desc, productInfo, table?)`: skin tone = nearest of 8 tones from `HeadColor` (a grey,
  unsaturated head is a blank or classic default and just snaps; a coloured head far from every tone
  is non-human); colour sources in priority: body colours, item-name colour words, the curated table;
  dominant = the most saturated candidate (shirt weighted 1.2); shirt and pants colours; a derived eye
  colour; neon list; hair (layered or rigid, with `hairIsLayered`), one head accessory, the signature
  accessory (always rigid, for the animal socket); bizarre reasons (`nonhuman_skin`, `neon`,
  `many_accessories` >= 6, `extreme_scale`, `nonhuman_bundle` by name words, `oversized_accessory`)
  and the one signature feature (`neon_tips`, `oversized_accessory`, `nonhuman_bundle`,
  `patterned_fur`, `none`); the name hit rate.
- `compose("human", stock, traits)` returns appearance properties only (skin tone index and colour,
  face id or 0 with a stock expression for a dynamic head, hair, else one head accessory): brief v2
  §13a, no tint, no accents, nothing about size.
- `compose(animal, stock, traits)`: fur = `clampPalette(mix(stock.body, dominant, 0.3))`, pattern from
  the shirt colour, tail tip from the pants colour, eyes derived (the rodent's locked bloodshot eyes
  never change, tested), one rigid accessory for `HeadSocket`, at most one softened neon accent
  (ear tips and tail), nothing about hitbox, hip height or scale (tested).

## Decisions this settles or amends (for the lead; D13, D22)

1. **Face path:** `Face` is 0 and the head is dynamic for every sampled account, so the human 30 %
   face comes from (a) `desc.Face ~= 0`: `InsertService:LoadAsset(face)` -> the Decal onto `FaceDecal`
   (works, measured), else (b) the dynamic head maps to one of the 6 stock expressions (D13 already
   says so). The "copy the Decal from `CreateHumanoidModelFromDescription`" wording is dead; the
   model is still the source for **rigid accessories** (works) and for reading `FaceControls`.
   Whether our custom R15 bodies should carry the player's dynamic head itself (the created model's
   `Head` MeshPart with `FaceControls`, `WrapTarget` and the mesh/texture ids is right there) is a W1
   question: it would give the real face at the cost of a moving part outside the shared UV set.
2. **Colour words hit 42 %**, so `data/blend/item_colours.json` (the curated table) and the body
   colours are not a fallback but the main source; item names add signal for about 4 in 10 items.
3. **Hair is usually layered** now (3 of the 4 hair items seen are WrapLayer): humans take it as-is
   (an Accessory on an R15 body with `WrapTarget`s), animals never do (`signatureAccessory` is rigid).
4. **Rate and cache:** cold fetches are 15-33 ms and repeats are cached inside the server, so the
   AvatarBlendService can fetch on possession without a queue; 49 joins cost about 1 s of web time.
5. `GetProductInfo` fails with HTTP 400 on old or removed assets (10 %): treat as "no info", not an error.
6. Nothing in Luau Execution needed a saved place version: inject mode carried the suite.

## What W1 should carry forward

- `AvatarBlendService.apply`: description -> `BlendRules.traits` (with `GetProductInfo` cache and the
  curated table) -> `compose` -> `BodyFactory.applyLook` (skin part colours, `FaceDecal` via LoadAsset
  or stock expression, hair accessory, `HeadSocket` weld with the x0.45 shrink); revert on release.
- Keep the two classic face ids and the 16 UserIds as the `Avatar` engine suite's fixed set
  (§6.5), and add the owner's dev-avatar samples to `data/blend/dev_avatar_samples.json` from
  `build/reports/avatar_api2.json` (the descriptions are there in plain data).
- Decide the dynamic-head question (1) before OS-6b's body import.
