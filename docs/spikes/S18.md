# S18 Paintball hit registration with lag compensation, in the town server

**Verdict: GO.** Paintball can share the town server (D29 primary design). The TeleportService and
reserved-server fallback is **not needed**.

## Question
Can the server run paintballs at 60 Hz with travel and drop, a five-ball magazine, a 1 s pose history
and lag-compensated hit tests, beside the full town population? The arena must add under 1.5 ms per
frame for 16 players and 10 bots. Server hits must agree with the shooter's view for at least 95 % of
shots at 150 ms. (BUILD_PLAN §4.3 S18, D29, `arena.json`.)

## What was built
| Module | Role |
|---|---|
| `src/shared/Paintball/Config.luau` | `arena.json` values in studs: speed 90 st/s, drop 9.8 st/s², ttl 1.5 s, 5 balls, 0.25 s between shots, 1.6 s reload, 1 s history at 60 Hz, maximum rewind 0.4 s, splat pool 400, body capsule r 1.2 from 1.0 to 4.4 st |
| `src/shared/Paintball/Ballistics.luau` | Analytic flight `o + v t - g t²/2` (the client predicts the exact same ball), segment vs capsule, segment vs box (unrolled, no allocation), and an aim solution that compensates the drop |
| `src/shared/Paintball/Magazine.luau` | Fire interval, automatic reload after the last ball, manual reload (R) only when not full, no firing while reloading |
| `src/server/Paintball/PoseHistory.luau` | A ring of (t, x, y, z) per body in flat arrays. The 1 s window holds 62 samples. Sampling interpolates linearly and clamps to the window |
| `src/server/Paintball/Projectiles.luau` | Server balls stepped at 60 Hz against the boxes, and against each enemy capsule sampled at `now - rewind` from its history. The first contact along the step wins and leaves a splat record. The pose sampler is injectable |
| `src/server/Paintball/Splats.luau` | A ring pool capped at 400 that overwrites the oldest record. Each record is a wall splat (position and normal) or a body splat (body id) |
| `src/server/Paintball/Layout.luau` | Greybox: a 120 × 120 st box, 5 floors 14 st apart, a 30 × 30 atrium through floors 1-4, 4 pillars per floor, walls and roof (45 boxes), and a stairwell per storey in rotating corners. Also a waypoint graph |
| `src/server/Paintball/Harness.luau` | Stub players, which walk, strafe and use the stairs, and bots, which walk a waypoint brain, take the stairs and shoot the nearest enemy on their floor. Every player shot is flown three ways (below) |

**How a shot is judged.** Every player shot is flown three ways:
- **truth**: the shooter's actual view. Targets are interpolated from 20 Hz snapshots at this shot's actual delay: RTT × (1 ± 12 % jitter) plus a 50 ms interpolation buffer.
- **server**: the 60 Hz history, rewound to the shooter's timestamp. The client stamps each shot with the server time of the frame it was showing; the server clamps that stamp to its own estimate (an EWMA of jittered RTT samples plus the interpolation delay) ± 75 ms. This is the plan's "rewound to the shooter's timestamp". An "estimate only" mode is measured as well.
- **naive**: no rewind, for contrast.

A shot "agrees" when the server names the same body as the truth, or names none when the truth names none.

## Numbers against the plan's criteria

- The S5 town ran in the same frames: 102 actors and 45 vehicles, 147 puppets moved with BulkMoveTo on their roots.
- The arena had 16 stub players and 10 bots as 26 Humanoid puppets.
- Each condition ran 1,800 frames (30 s) at 60 Hz.

In each condition:
- Balls fired: 945-1,019, of which 356-391 were bot shots.
- About 180 automatic reloads and 7-15 manual reloads.

- 0 balls had their rewind clamped, and 0 bodies were stuck.

Memory went from 401 MB to 413 MB (`GetTotalMemoryUsageMb`), and the Luau heap was 5.0 MB. The whole task took 3.4 s of engine time.

Unit tests in `tests/unit/paintball_hitreg.spec.luau` (9 cases) cover:
- the magazine and the reload paths;
- the 4.9 st drop over 1 s;
- the aim solution;
- the capsule and box tests;
- the history ring (span, interpolation, clamping);
- the splat cap;
- a rewound ball hitting where a naive ball misses;
- the harness at 150 ms: >= 200 shots, >= 95 % agreement, splats capped, no stuck bodies;
- estimate-only rewind still at >= 95 % with ±12 % jitter.

## What this does not cover (it needs the owner, OS-5b)
Luau Execution runs no physics, no players and no replication. Still unmeasured:
- how it feels: client prediction, locked first person, sprint and crouch;
- the bandwidth of `Paintball.State` at 20 Hz;
- network-owned player characters, whose positions in the real game reach the server through replication, not the harness.

## Carry-forward for W4 #10
- **Rewind to the client's timestamp, clamped to the server's estimate ± 75 ms.** Estimate-only rewind falls below 95 % once RTT jitter passes about ±20 % at 150 ms. Send `renderTime` (the server time of the interpolated frame being shown) with every shot through `Paintball.Input`.
- **History pushes.** Push `PoseHistory` for every arena body on `Heartbeat`, from the replicated character root for players and from the motor for bots. The history belongs with `MoveValidator`'s rings in W1 #6, as the plan says.
- **Body tests.** `Projectiles` returns the closest-approach parameter for body hits, which is good to within one 1.5 st step. If walls and bodies need exact ordering, return the entry parameter instead.
- **Bots.** They use the D25 borrowed bodies. The harness's waypoint brain is a placeholder for `PaintballBot` on `Nav`.
- **Splats.** Records replicate as `Splat` events, and the client keeps its own 400-decal pool.
- **Squirrels and the rodent.** Keep them out of the target list (D29: squirrels excluded).
- **Regression baseline for the `Paintball` suite (§6.5):** arena 0.18 ms/frame avg, 0.31 ms p99, beside the S5 town at 0.25 ms. Fail on a 3× regression.
