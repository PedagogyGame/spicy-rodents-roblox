# S12: Headless renders (Blender in the container)

**Question** (BUILD_PLAN §4.3 S12). Can the container render the turntables the owner approves art
from (G2, OS-3) without a GPU: one 36-frame turntable of a test squirrel at 512 px with Cycles (CPU)
and EEVEE (if it starts without a GPU)? Pass: minutes per frame recorded; a turntable page the owner
can open. Fallback: Cycles at lower samples; a Workbench preview; G2 from still renders only.

**Verdict: GO.** Cycles CPU renders the 36-frame turntable in 8.1 minutes wall time on 2 threads
(13.5 s per frame at 64 samples with denoising, 0.23 min/frame). EEVEE does not start on the stock
container (no `libEGL`), but after installing Mesa's software EGL it renders headlessly too (about
12 s per frame at 16 samples). The page `docs/renders/squirrel_turntable.html` is the deliverable.

## Method

- `tools/blender/squirrel_test_rig.py`: builds the test squirrel in bpy 5.2.3 LTS (the venv at
  `/root/.cache/spicy-rodents/venv`): 13 bones that are a subset of the FINAL `RigSpec` names
  (`Root`, `Hips`, `Spine1`, `Chest`, `Neck`, `Head`, `Tail1`-`Tail3`, `FrontL_Upper`, `FrontR_Upper`,
  `BackL_Upper`, `BackR_Upper`), 2,864 triangles of ellipsoids skinned by distance (2 influences per
  vertex), three materials named after the rig regions (`Body` fur `#b78453` = stock fur 0, `Pattern`
  belly and tail tip, `Eyes`), one 20-frame `Run` action. 1 Blender unit = 1 stud (Unit System None),
  body along -Y, +Z up, shoulder about 1.2 studs. Ideas reused from
  `/root/feasibility/blender/rig_export_test.py` (which proved FBX/glTF export of such a rig).
- `tools/blender/render_turntable.py --engine CYCLES|EEVEE|WORKBENCH --samples N --frames 36 --res 512 --threads 2`:
  studio world, ground disc, three area lights, AgX view transform, camera on a pivot that turns 10
  degrees per frame; the rig holds `Run` frame 5. Writes `frame_NN.png` and `timing.json` per frame.
- `tools/blender/contact_sheet.py` (system Python with Pillow): 6 x 6 contact sheet, 256 px page
  frames, three 512 px hero stills, and the HTML turntable page (drag to spin, auto-rotate).
- `tools/blender/run_s12.sh`: the driver, pinned with `taskset -c 2,3` and `render.threads = 2` so
  the other builder kept two cores. Ran in the background while S5 and S9 were built.

## Measured

| Engine | Starts headless? | Samples | Res | Threads | s / frame (mean, min-max) | min / frame | 36 frames |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cycles CPU, adaptive, OIDN denoise | yes | 64 | 512 | 2 | 13.5 (11.8-15.1) | 0.23 | 8.1 min |
| Cycles CPU, no denoise (smoke) | yes | 8 | 512 | 2 | 1.7 | 0.03 | about 1 min |
| Cycles CPU, no denoise (smoke) | yes | 4 | 256 | 2 | 0.35 | 0.006 | 13 s |
| EEVEE (`BLENDER_EEVEE`, stock container) | **no**: `Couldn't open libEGL.so.1`, process aborts (SIGABRT, exit 134) before any Python handler runs | 16 | 512 | 2 | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| EEVEE after `apt-get install libegl1 libgles2 libgl1 libglx-mesa0 libgl1-mesa-dri` with `EGL_PLATFORM=surfaceless LIBGL_ALWAYS_SOFTWARE=1` | **yes** (Mesa llvmpipe) | 16 | 512 | 2 | 12.0 (3 frames: 12.2, 12.2, 11.9) | 0.20 | about 7.2 min (extrapolated) |
| Workbench (stock container) | no: same `libEGL` abort | 8 | 512 | 2 | n/a | n/a | n/a |

Scene setup (rig build plus lights and camera) takes 0.26 s. Cycles' denoiser is most of the 64-sample
cost: without it 64 samples would land near 4-5 s per frame (from the 8-sample point). Output sizes:
36 page frames at 256 px plus the sheet and three stills = 2.4 MB under `docs/renders/`
(the 512 px frames stay in `build/blender/cycles/`, gitignored).

Note on `timing.json` for the aborted engines: the abort kills the interpreter, so the script's own
failure recording never runs; the driver's exit code (134) and the log line are the record. The
Mesa libraries were installed only in this session's container; `docs/ENVIRONMENT_SETUP.sh` should
add them if W5 wants EEVEE previews (Cycles alone is enough for G2).

## What W1 / W5 should carry forward

1. **Cycles CPU at 32-64 samples with denoising is the turntable path**: about 8 minutes per rig for
   36 frames on 2 threads, 4 minutes on all 4 cores. Budget one turntable per asset per review round;
   render in the background while code work continues (as done here).
2. **EEVEE needs Mesa**: add the five packages and the two environment variables to
   `docs/ENVIRONMENT_SETUP.sh` if fast previews matter; it is not faster than Cycles under llvmpipe, so
   Cycles stays primary. Workbench would need the same libraries.
3. **Keep PNG budgets small**: page frames at 256 px, one sheet, three stills; keep `docs/renders/`
   under 15 MB in total (2.4 MB now). Full-size frames live in `build/`.
4. The 13-bone test rig is a stand-in: the real squirrel uses the 35-bone `RigSpec.squirrel` contract
   (Tail1-8, four segments per leg) and the `Body`/`Pattern`/`Eyes`/`FurCards` region MeshParts; the
   builder here already names its materials after those regions so the tint zones can be checked in a
   render before S8(c2) lands.
5. Render settings worth keeping: AgX view transform, three area lights (90/30/60 W), neutral grey world
   at 0.5, 50 mm lens 4.2 units from the pivot; camera orbit by rotating a pivot empty.
