# S6 Bake, data and terrain

Date: 2026-11-09. Branch `w0-foundation`. CI place versions 5-9 (unpublished saves; final runs on **v9**).
Verdict: **GO** (terrain is the primary-ground candidate for G0; S11 still decides far-view rendering).

## Question

Can the town be baked from `generated/` data in Lune into `build/Town.rbxm` fast enough, with the same result in the engine (BakeParity)? Can the server write the ground as Terrain from the verbatim v5.4 height function within budget, including the near-hill backdrop bands? Do the largest generated data chunks require fast enough?

## Method

1. `lune run tests/run.luau world_` (golden + bake specs), then `lune run tools/bake/main.luau`, `rojo build ci.project.json -o build/test.rbxl` (Town.rbxm mapped to `Workspace.Town`), `save_place --place ci`.
2. `run_engine_tests tools/cloud/tasks/world_build.luau --version 9`: lot and road counts against `ReplicatedStorage.Shared.Generated`, 3-D overlap of every solid lot greybox from engine CFrame/Size, the plan hash recomputed in the engine, the structural instance hash of the placed Town and of a fresh engine-side `Bake.run`, the geometry delta between them, and the first-require time of every Generated chunk.
3. `run_engine_tests tools/cloud/tasks/world_terrain.luau --version 9 --timeout 300s`: one task writes the playable rectangle from `World/Height` (river water and mud bed, 99 road rectangles painted Asphalt/Concrete/Cobblestone/Slate), then the near-hill bands, timed separately, then reads voxel columns back.
   - Bands are a stand-in: Terrain 0-2,000 st beyond the south and west play edges (owner decision 7 default: hills south and west; worldgen_districts_art §14.4 asks for 0.5-2 km, the 0-0.5 km apron is written too so the ground is continuous), rising from the edge height to rolling hills of 60-200 st (top voxel 240 st).

## Findings

- **Instance hashes across runtimes must not include float geometry.** The first engine run failed parity: hashing Size/CFrame rounded to 1e-2 st flipped 4 of 2,128 lines between Lune and the placed Town (LookVector 0.9995 boundary after the engine re-orthonormalises CFrames) and 131 lines between Lune and an engine-side bake (positions such as 48.675 land on both sides of float32 rounding). Fix: `BakeHash` hashes the plan's doubles at 1e-3 (identical in both runtimes), `InstanceHash` hashes structure only, and geometry is compared with a tolerance (`maxGeometryDelta`, measured 9.5e-6 st).
- **The data has no explicit parent links.** 15 lots lie inside a building (6 galleria shops and the atrium; 8 hospital rooms). The bake derives them (`Plan.containment`) and makes them non-solid interiors; W1's lot schema should carry `parent` explicitly (invariant 1, "no footprint overlap except declared children").
- **One canopy overlaps its building** (hospital porte-cochere 1.6 st into the hospital): trimmed to the wall by `Plan.attachSlabs` with `AttachedTo`. All other solid footprints are disjoint.
- **Heights are not in the data.** Kits carry greybox heights read off the v5.4 notes; W1's `data/lot_types/*.json` should own them.
- **Footprints are world-axis boxes**, so non-cardinal yaws (62 lots: trees, details, signs, shelters) keep an axis-aligned box and only their front snaps to a quarter turn.
- **WriteVoxels is cheap; Luau table filling is two-thirds of the cost.** 50 M voxels in about 5 s in one Luau Execution task with no players. Not measured here (needs Studio or a live server): client meshing, replication and streaming of that terrain (S11, OS-1c), and physics collision building on first contact.

## Carry-forward for W1

1. `WorldBoot` writes Terrain with `TerrainBake.write` at server boot when `Terrain.BakeHash` is stale: about 1 s for the town and 3-4 s for near bands, so no chunk-around-players ordering is needed. Keep `yieldEvery = 1`.
2. Add `parent` (children) and greybox `height` (or storeys) to the lot schema; drop `Plan.containment`'s inference and the kit height table once the data has them.
3. Author `terrain_extensions.json` (hill, ridge, back field, long beach; D32) as patches over `World/Height`, then re-run `world_terrain` (budget headroom: about 20 s).
4. Roads are 40-st pitched segments; v5.4 draws 104 terrain-following segments per grid street. Cross-slope and junction blending are W1 Road builder work; terrain under axis roads is already painted with the road material.
5. `Ground.groundAt` covers terrain and the bridge deck only; pavement offsets, mall floors, platforms and roofs arrive as baked collidable geometry (Colliders) in W1.
6. BakeParity: compare plan hashes exactly, structure by hash, geometry by tolerance. Do not hash float32 geometry across runtimes.
7. Re-run S6 on the extended footprint (D32) together with S11.
