# The shared golf carts, mopeds and scooters: what to look at (10 minutes)

You asked on 22 November: "lets add the drivability to all players, for a scattered restricted total of 3 golf carts, 2 mopeds, and 5 electric scooters, anyone can take them or use them, and you do not get tickets or problems with the police with them. prepare most carefully, the dynamics, physics and scene of being hit by a ..."

The end of that message was cut off. I read it as: people try to get out of the way of one coming at them; anyone it still hits is knocked aside, stumbles or falls, and gets back up. Nothing worse, and no blood.

Nothing has been uploaded. All of it is built from plain Roblox parts, like the bikes.

They replace the two golf carts that used to be parked in the town centre. The patrol cars, the pickup, the SUV, the two bikes, the jet ski and the drone have not changed.

Every spot is checked by a test:
- it is on a lawn, a path, a plaza or a pavement, never in a lane the town's cars drive;
- it is not inside a building, a fence or the river;
- nothing baked into the town stands in it or over it;
- the ground is level.

The ten are at least 100 studs apart. When the town starts, 53 of its 78 people are within 250 studs of one. That is about ten seconds at a sprint.

## How to ride

- **Who can ride:** anyone in a person's body. A squirrel or the Spicy Rodent cannot.
- **Getting on:** walk up to one. A small **"E ride the scooter"** label shows over the nearest free one, then press **E**. On a phone, the label is a **RIDE** button. While you ride, a **GET OFF** button sits above the jump button.
- **No key and no rental.** Nobody owns them.
- **Getting off:** press **E** again. It stays where you left it. If it is left away from its spot, untouched for 5 minutes, and nobody is near it or its spot, it quietly goes back.
- **No police trouble.** Riding one never gives a ticket, a wanted level or a chase, whatever the rules are for other vehicles.

## What happens when you bump into someone

**People see you coming.** If a townsperson, a shop worker or an officer is in your way, they hop out of it: a quick step sideways, their arms up for a moment. Now and then they say something, like "Whoa! Careful!" or "Hey, slow down!". They step only where the way is clear, so never into a wall. Most people get out of the way. About one in seven freezes.

**Anyone you still hit:**
- **Slowly (under 9 studs/s):** they stagger. Their arms go up and they are shoved a step aside. They are back on their way in under a second.
- **Faster:** they are knocked over, along the way you were going and off to the side. They are pushed about 3 to 5.5 studs, farther the faster you were going. They sit on the ground for 1.5 to 3 seconds, longer the faster you were going. Then they get up and walk on.
- They may say something like "Oof!" or "Whoa! I'm okay, I'm okay."
- You feel the bump: your vehicle loses almost half its speed.
- The same person is not knocked again for 6 seconds.
- Nobody is ever hurt, taken to hospital, killed or sent back to the menu.

**Another player in a person's body:** they are knocked the same way. On their own screen their body tips over in that direction, lies there for the same time, then stands up. Their controls come back when they are up. They see "A golf cart knocked you over. Up you get!"

**A squirrel:** the rule that cars run squirrels over is only for the town's traffic. These ten never run anyone over.
- A player's squirrel or the Spicy Rodent in the way is nudged a few studs aside and dazed for about a second. The player sees "A scooter bumped you aside. You're fine!"
- The computer's squirrels scurry off along their branches and fences.

## Cost (phones)

- **Parts:** a scooter is 15 parts, a moped 18 and a golf cart 40. The ten come to 231 parts, against 80 for the two old carts.
- **Shadows:** only the scooter's board, the moped's body and seat, the cart's big parts and the tyres cast shadows. That is 47 shadow parts for the ten.
- **Each frame:** the server checks only the shared vehicles that are moving, against the people and squirrels within 40 studs of each. That is about 1,000 simple sums a frame even if all ten are ridden at once, and nothing at all while none is moving.
- **Each phone:** the "E ride" label is looked for four times a second, among the ten.

## For you to look at in Studio

1. Walk to the scooter in the town centre. Does the "E ride" label show? Ride it.
   - Do you stand on the board?
   - Does it feel like a brisk run, and is it quick to turn?
2. Ride the moped from the corner store along the pavement, then the golf cart in the park.
3. Ride at a walking townsperson.
   - Do they hop aside in time?
   - Does someone you hit fall over, sit on the ground a moment and get up?
4. With a second player in a person's body, bump into them. Do they tip over and get up?
5. Leave a scooter somewhere far from its spot and walk away. About 5 minutes later, with nobody near, it should be back.

## Left for you to decide

- **The golf cart seats one.** The game has one seat per vehicle, so a passenger would need new work. **Default if you say nothing:** one rider.
- **Moped speed.** 34 studs/s is faster than a person's sprint, and slower than the patrol car. **Default if you say nothing:** 34.
- **About one person in seven freezes** rather than stepping aside. **Default if you say nothing:** that stays, so that a bump can still happen now and then.
