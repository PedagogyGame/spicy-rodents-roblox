# Regular squirrels: what to try

You asked: "the regular squirrel should not be ran away from by npcs, unless it jumps right on them or at them, and make sure those regular squirrels can be run over by cars, if they are not careful".

Nobody has seen this on a screen yet. The checks behind it are headless.

## 1. People and your regular squirrel

Pick **Squirrel** (not the Spicy Rodent) and go among people.

| Try | What should happen |
|---|---|
| Walk up to someone, stand in front of them, follow them, sprint past them, brush against them | Nothing. They keep walking their way. Nobody runs, backs away, shouts for help or calls the police. |
| Sit on a wire, a fence or a branch over their heads | Nothing. Nobody stops to look up or point. |
| Stay near people for a while | Now and then someone turns their head to you for a moment. About one glance in four comes with a friendly line ("Aw, a squirrel.", "Look at that fluffy tail!"). They keep walking. |
| Jump so that you land on someone, or fly past their head | They start: the arms come up, a yelp ("Whoa!", "Hey! Not on me!"), two quick steps away from you. Then they walk on as before. No alarm, no police, no crowd. |
| Jump straight at someone and land just short of their feet (within about 3.5 studs) | The same start. |
| Jump past someone a few studs to the side, or over them from wire to wire | Nothing. |
| Jump on the same person again straight away | Nothing: one start per person every 20 seconds. |
| Bounce from person to person down a street | At most three people start in any 10 seconds. |
| Jump on a cyclist, a shop worker, a police officer | They yelp and flinch. The cyclist rides on, the shop worker stops for a moment, the officer carries on. |

**The Spicy Rodent is unchanged.** People still run from it, the duel, the vigilantes, the police and the looking-up from below all work as before. An infected squirrel still frightens people too.

## 2. Cars and your regular squirrel

| Try | What should happen |
|---|---|
| Stand or run in the road in front of a moving car, van, truck, RV, trolley, patrol car or ambulance | You are run over: "RUN OVER!" and back to the menu, as before. Cars do not slow down for a squirrel. |
| Sit on a wire, a branch or a roof over the road while cars pass under | Nothing. |
| Stand in front of a motorcycle | Nothing: motorcycles run nobody over (as before). |

## 3. The computer's squirrels and the roads (new)

Stay near a street with trees on both sides and traffic (the main north-south road, the boulevard) and watch the squirrels.

| Watch for | What should happen |
|---|---|
| A squirrel crossing | It comes down its tree, runs to the kerb, stops and looks for a second or two, dashes across and climbs a tree on the other side. |
| A squirrel at the kerb with a car coming | It waits for the car to pass. If no gap comes in about 9 seconds it goes back up its tree. |
| A squirrel racing a car | Now and then one misjudges a fast car and dashes at the last moment. Some scrape through. Most are hit. |
| A squirrel that is hit | A squeak, a puff of pale dust. It lies still on its side for 3 seconds and fades away. Nothing is left on the road. |
| Later | Within about a minute and a half a squirrel is back in town on a tree. The number of squirrels in town does not go down. |

These only happen near a player. Never more than two squirrels are knocked flat in any minute, in the whole town.

## Questions for you (each has a default built in)

1. **How often should a squirrel race a car?** Default: rarely. In a ten-minute test with a player beside every one of the town's 14 squirrels, 55 crossed and 2 were knocked flat. With one player in town I expect far fewer near them, perhaps one in half an hour (an estimate, not measured). It is one number to change.
2. **Lying on its side, then fading:** is that mild enough, or would you rather it just vanishes in the puff?
3. **Motorcycles** run nobody over (the old game's rule). Should they?
4. **The lounge's guards and the yard dogs** still go for any squirrel, regular or not (the guards knock it out at the door, the dogs chase it and bark). I left both as they are.
