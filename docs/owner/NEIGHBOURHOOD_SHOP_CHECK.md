# The Neighbourhood Shop: what to look at (10 minutes)

Your second store is in the town. Your interior and your gable roof are used as you made them; I built the outside walls, the shopfront sign, the steps and the lights around them. Nothing in it is sold, sat on or worn yet: it is to look at and walk through. Everything below has been tested without a screen. Nobody has seen it inside Roblox yet, so this list is the part only you can judge.

## Where it is
On **Main Street** (the wide boulevard with the trolley line), on its **north side, at the old-town end**, about 90 studs from the trolley stop. It faces south across the street, so its front gets the sun. In the world it stands at x 686, z 70; it is number 17 on the town map (`docs/owner/files/town_map.png`).

## How to get there
1. Pick **Townsperson** (or Police) from the menu. Only people go inside; squirrels can run over the roof.
2. Walk to Main Street and along its north sidewalk toward the old town. Look for a teal shopfront with big windows under a grey gable roof with a chimney, and **THE NEIGHBOURHOOD SHOP** in cream letters on a dark board over the windows.
3. Walk up the three steps and in through the open door (on the right of the shopfront).
