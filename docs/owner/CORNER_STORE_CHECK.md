# Pepper's Corner Store: what to try (15 minutes)

The corner store from your brief is built: a 1970s-feel shop (not a period piece) with a working till and all 22 things on its shelves, each one usable (the dog treats came back on 18 November, with the town's yard dogs: see below). Everything below has been tested without a screen. Nobody has seen it inside Roblox yet, so this list is the part only you can judge. Pictures: `docs/renders/corner_store/` (start with `contact_sheet.jpg`).

## Where it is
At the west end of the east shopping strip, x 957, z 84, facing south toward the road. It is labelled "corner store" on the town map (`docs/owner/files/town_map.png`). Look for a brick shop with big windows, a metal canopy and a red sign, **PEPPER'S CORNER STORE**, on the parapet. There are three ways in: the double doorway at the front (up two steps), a side door on the west wall, and a service door at the back.

## Who can shop
Only people. A squirrel or the Spicy Rodent can run in, but the shelves tell them "Only people shop here."

## The yard dogs (for the dog treats)
Eight friendly dogs made of plain parts, one to a yard: three at the town's doghouses (one in the old town core, two in the north gardens), one behind the Victorian house in the old town core, and four behind the west side's big houses (where v5.4 kept its dogs on a lead). The five yards without a doghouse have a small wooden kennel (solid to people and squirrels; the dog walks round it). v5.4's fourth doghouse stands in Sycamore Park, not in a yard, so it has no dog.

| # | What to try | What you should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walk up to a yard as a person | The dog ambles about its yard, a new spot every few seconds, never out of it |
| 2 | Run into the yard as a squirrel or the Spicy Rodent | The dog runs at you and barks (silent until the lead picks a library bark); you read "WOOF! A yard dog chases you out of its yard." It barks only once you are in the yard (just outside it runs to the fence), never bites and never follows you out. Up on a fence or a branch, it ignores you. Only players' squirrels and the Spicy Rodent meet the dogs: the computer's squirrels run their trees and wires, and none of their routes crosses a dog's yard on the ground |
| 3 | Bring the Spicy Rodent near people by a yard | The people near the bark notice the rodent and run, as when they spot it themselves (v5.4: a bark put people on alert) |
| 4 | Give a dog a treat (above) | It sits and wags; a squirrel can cross its yard for two minutes |
| 5 | At night (after 19:30 game time) | Each dog lies down in front of its doghouse or kennel |

**What they cost:** 96 parts for the eight dogs and 20 for the five kennels, no meshes, no shadows. A dog thinks twice a second and moves 15 times a second, and only while a player is within 150 studs of it; otherwise it stands still and costs nothing.

**Questions for you (each has a default built in):**
1. **Where the dogs live.** I put them in eight yards that had room; the park's doghouse has none (it is in the park, not a yard). Say if you want a dog there or anywhere else.
2. **How mild the scare is.** A dog chases and barks; a player's squirrel is told to clear off but is not pushed or hurt (the squirrel's own movement is the player's, so a push would fight it). Say if you want a push, or a dog that can knock a squirrel out like the lounge's guards.
3. **No cats yet.** The brief's yard cats (a telegraphed swat) are not built.

## What is not finished
- **The kite** flies and steers the basic way (left, right, string out and in), as you asked: no dives or loops, no wind that changes, no string that can tangle or snap. Its string is a line drawn from your hand. You stand still while it is up (the keys steer it instead of walking).
- **Sounds** are all named and wired but silent until the lead picks library sounds (no uploads).
- **The pictures** in `docs/renders/corner_store/` were taken when the dog treats were first placed, so they show the box on the third gondola as it is now.
- Nothing is saved between servers (like the wallet): what you bought lasts while the server runs.
