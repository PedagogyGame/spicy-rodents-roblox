# What came in on 18 November, and what I did with it

You sent three zips. Everything is kept in the repository:
- the finished pinball models are in `docs/reference/pinball_art/`;
- the whack-a-mole project is in `docs/reference/whack_a_mole/`.

## 1. The finished pinball models (from ChatGPT): accepted, with one redo and one fix

I ran my own copy of the checker, not the one inside the zip. **All 24 files pass:**
- every name the game needs is there;
- every pivot is within 1 mm;
- every outline the ball touches is within 3 mm;
- everything is in metres;
- every file is inside its triangle budget.

Each machine comes to about 15,000 triangles, which is light enough for phones. The pictures look right: walnut and brass, red and black, blue and silver, with the artwork in place.

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| 21 GLB models (cabinet, flipper, bumper, plunger, gate, and each machine's playfield, guides, slingshots, targets, spinner, ramps, pod hoods) | **Accepted.** They are going into the game in place of the plain parts. |
| Playfield and apron artwork (inside the GLBs) | **Accepted, with a fix.** A few painted words overlap: THE WIRE over SUPER, RAMP over the letter F, HOT LANE under the ramp. The positions came from my layout, not from ChatGPT, so I am fixing the positions and redrawing those boards. |
| 3 backglass pictures | **Redone.** Bright horizontal stripes cut across the artwork where the text bands were dimmed and the gaps between them were not. I am rebuilding all three from ChatGPT's own original emblems, dimmed evenly. |
| Blender sources, previews, audit scripts | **Kept for reference only.** The game does not use them. |
| `Pinball_ChatGPT_Pack.zip` | Identical to the pack I gave you, so it is not stored twice. |

## 2. Whack-a-mole: the game is accepted; the model is too heavy as it is

**Where it goes:** the bowling alley's games room, as the tenth minigame.

**The rules, as your browser version plays them:**
- five holes;
- a 45-second round;
- 10 points for a hit and 2 off for a miss;
- the score never goes below zero.

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| The look: red and gold cabinet, green deck, five friendly moles, the mallet, the WHACK A MOLE sign with SCORE and TIME windows | **Accepted.** |
| `Whack_A_Mole.glb` as it is | **Not used directly.** It is 232,000 triangles in 246 pieces, about 15 times what one pinball machine costs, and it would make the alley lag on phones. I am making a game version from your Blender file: same look, about 12,000 triangles, the fixed parts merged into a few pieces, and the five moles and the mallet kept separate so they can move. |
| Coin slot and coin return | **Changed to a FREE PLAY plate**, as on the pinball machines: no coins, which keeps the mild rating. |
| The Blender demo animation, the Blender play script, the browser game | **Kept for reference.** The game runs its own version of the same rules, so it works with mouse, touch and gamepad, and with other players watching. |
