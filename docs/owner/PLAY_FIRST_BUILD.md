# Play the first build (9 November 2026)

File: `docs/owner/files/SpicyRodents_play.rbxl` (about 1.3 MB). Nothing gets published. It runs on your computer in Studio.

## 5 steps
1. **Open it.** In Roblox Studio, choose File > Open from File and pick `SpicyRodents_play.rbxl`. You see the town in the editor: grey-box houses, roads and shops. There is no grass yet.
2. **Press Play** (the triangle, or F5). A dark-green loading screen says SPICY RODENTS and has an orange progress bar. The text under the bar says what is being built: the sky, then the ground, river and hills, then the cars, then the 102 people and animals. Next, "Preparing the climbing routes on this computer". This takes about 5 to 20 seconds.
3. **Pick who to play.** A menu asks "Who do you want to be?" and shows five big buttons: **Spicy Rodent, Squirrel, Townsperson, Police, Police drone**. Click one and the camera moves to that body.
4. **Play.** The panel at the top left always lists the keys for your current role.
   - **Spicy Rodent / Squirrel:** WASD runs (the camera sets the direction) and Shift sprints. Run into a tree, pole or downspout and keep holding W to climb. A/D chooses the way at a fork, Space leaps, Q drops and E grabs.
     - The rodent also pounces with a left click and **bites anyone it touches**. The person shouts, runs away, and your bite count and Power go up (top centre).
     - Cars kill you: you see **RUN OVER!** and the menu comes back.
   - **Townsperson:** WASD walks, Shift sprints and Space jumps. **F kicks**: the rodent has 100 health and a kick does 55, so two kicks knock it out.
   - **Police:** you start next to the patrol car. Walk up, press **E** to get in, drive with WASD, and press E to get out.
   - **Police drone:** it launches by itself. WASD flies, Space goes up, Q goes down, Shift boosts, and E lands.
5. **Switch or stop.** The orange **Menu (M)** button (top right) or the M key brings the menu back at any time. If you get stuck in a wall or the river, use Esc > Reset: it moves you to a safe spot. Press Stop (Shift+F5) to end.

## Not working yet (please tell me what you notice)
- **Cars are see-through to you.** AI cars are drawn on your computer only. Your patrol car drives through them, and they only hurt squirrels and the rodent.
- **No animations for kicks, bites or the animals' legs.** You get on-screen messages instead. People use the stock Roblox walk.
- **Nobody has tested the feel yet.** Movement, the camera, climbing, driving and the drone are new on screen and have only been tested headless. If something feels wrong, say what you pressed and what happened.
- **The day stays fixed** at a sunny afternoon. The day/night clock comes in the next wave.
- **The town is grey boxes.** Real art comes later.
- **One Spicy Rodent per town.** With two players in a test, the second player who picks the rodent is told someone else already is.
- If the loading screen turns **red**, the server failed to start. Copy the text from View > Output and send it to me.
