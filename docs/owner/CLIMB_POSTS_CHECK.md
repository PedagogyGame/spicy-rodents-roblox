# Climbable light posts: what to check (D35)

You asked: *"make all trees climbable, and even all light posts"*. This sheet is the light posts (the trees are
being done separately).

## What changed

**Every light post in the town can be climbed: 164 posts.**

| Where | Posts | With an arm to the lamp head |
|---|---|---|
| The town's street lamps (every street and the stone bridge) | 133 | 133 |
| The two porch-light posts in the old town's yards | 2 | - |
| Chapel Street (the street out to the new venues) | 16 | - |
| The bowling alley's forecourt (either side of the ramp) | 2 | - |
| Bowl Lane | 6 | 6 |
| Lift Road, the car park's trailhead and the sky lift station | 5 | 5 |

How it plays, the same as a tree:

- **Getting on:** run at a post and keep W held, or press **E** next to it (for example while running past it along
  the sidewalk).
- **Climbing:** W goes up, S comes down, at the same speed as climbing a tree trunk.
- **The top is a perch:** holding W you stop at the very top of the post, on its cap.
- **The arm:** on the street lamps, press **A or D** on the post to pick the arm, then hold W: the squirrel runs out
  along the curved arm and sits on top of the lamp head.
- **Getting off:** Space leaps off (from the top it is a long leap down), or come down to the bottom and press Q to let
  go. On the stone bridge the posts stand next to the bridge's railing: E from the post jumps onto the railing.
- Street lamps are kept well away from telephone poles and trees, so from most tops there is nothing else close
  enough to jump to; you land on the ground (or, with a long leap and E in the air, whatever branch or wire you reach).

**Nothing about the lamps' look changed**: no parts were added (the climbs are invisible paths, like the trees' and
wires'), and the street lights still all come on together.

**Four lamp arms stop a little short of the head** (the main street, two lamps by the ice cream van's loop, and the
east-west street at z -174, two lamps by the ambulance route), because the lamp head there hangs over where those
vehicles drive. The squirrel runs most of the way out and stops before it is over the traffic.

**Not climbable:** the four lamp posts on the pier out in the sea (part of the far scenery past the edge of the town;
three are past the edge of the world).

## What it costs (phones)

- Parts: **0** added.
- Data: 308 invisible paths (164 climbs, 144 arms), about 6,500 path points, 6.7 % more than the town had; about
  220 KB of route data, loaded once.
- Per frame: nothing new runs; the squirrel uses the same climbing code as on trees.
