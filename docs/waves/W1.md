### What was built
- **#1 Util** (`src/shared/Util`): `Rng` (v5.4 mulberry32, bit-exact against V8), `Signal`, `Trove`, `Spring` (closed form), `SpatialHash`, `Geometry` (AABB/OBB, slab `segmentBoxEntry`), `Schema` (payload validators), `BufferCodec` (fixed little-endian layouts, q16), `Log` (levels, sink, rate limit).

- **#1 Net**: `Schemas` declares every §1.6 remote (34 action verbs, `Motor.State` 38-byte and `VehiclePose` 13-byte layouts). `Net` checks payloads against the schema and applies a per-player, per-remote token bucket. It drops dev-only remotes from non-devs and catches handler errors. Rejected packets are logged at a limited rate and dropped. It also has `fire`, `fireAll`, `fireNear`, buffer `stream`, and an engine binder `Net.engineServer`, which is never called on require.
- **#1 Clock**: `DayCurve` holds sunrise and sunset longer: 7 segments with tunable real-time shares, and both directions are invertible. `Clock` derives time from an epoch, a base and a rate; `setRate` re-anchors so time stays continuous, and clients mirror it through `anchor()`. `game()` returns day, hour, segment, week, winter, moon and raid week. Added `Calendar.raidWeek`.
- **#1 Data** (`src/shared/Data/init.luau`): lazy, chunk-aware accessors over `generated/`, plus `lot`, `lotType` (map or id), `routes()` as a `Routes/Registry` and `bakeHash`. A module the compiler has not produced yet reads as empty and is listed by `missing()`. `Data.default()` picks the engine `Shared.Generated` folder or Lune's `generated/`.
- **#4 Ladder** (`src/shared/Roles/Ladder.luau`, the only place the ladder is stated) covers:
  - rungs, subroles and side exits, with 35+7+1+5+1 = 49;
  - `params`, kept verbatim from §3.7;
  - 29 rule rows, each with an `id`;
  - the scores: squirrel, human queue (money through `EconomyRules.queueMoney`) and tier 2 (never money);
  - `rankOrder` (seniority tie-break, infection queue drop) and `candidates` (offerCascade; police recruitment ordered by seniority after the top-ranked human);
  - `keyHolders` (the top two drivers);
  - the rank policies in `applyRank`;
  - a pure, table-driven `resolve(event, ctx)` for every event, and `vacated`.
- **#4 RoleService** extends the S2 version and keeps its contract.
  - The 50th arrival is kicked before any body is assigned.
  - It joins players as lowest-rank humans, applies transitions and keeps a server-life `Wallet.purse` per member.
  - Offers: one live offer per rung, timeout, cascade, retry, and the choices Accept / Deer / Fox / Stay. Vacated scarce slots are offered in the same step.
  - It runs the idle release (D34): a warning at 750 s, release at 780 s, and `input` cancels it.
  - It also handles jail, top-two keys, `forceRole` (over-cap is logged) and `resetSession`.
- **Role Lab**: `RoleLab` is the dev-gated backend (become, stat, event, perf, tune, export, reset, state). `src/client/RoleLab/RoleLabClient` has a pure `model()` and `mount()` for the "Become X" panel.
- **#6 MovementService** (`src/server/Movement/MovementService.luau`), the rule 12 seam:
  - `onHandoff` calls `SweptCollision:markTeleported`, then `MoveValidator:reset`, then clears the pose history. Plug it into `Possession.new{ onHandoff = ... }`.
  - Squirrel and rodent packets go through the existing S3 `MoveValidator`. Other kinds get a per-kind speed cap.
  - Every body has a 1 s pose history (`Paintball/PoseHistory`).
  - It is log-only: corrections are withheld unless `Flags.moveValidatorEnforce` is on.
- **#6 Telemetry** moved from `src/server/Telemetry.luau` to `src/server/Telemetry/init.luau`; the instance path is unchanged. It records per-system ms per frame (mean, max and last), counts, remote bytes and `over(name, budget)`.
